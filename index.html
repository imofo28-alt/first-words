      How to use it with him
      • Together, on your lap. The app is a picture book; you are the teacher.
      • He swipes or taps; the app says the word in your voice. There are no wrong answers.
      • In the pause after the word, say it too. Afterwards, find the real thing: “Your ball! Same!”
      • Tap “Says it” once he uses a word away from the iPad. Short and daily beats long and rare.
      On the iPad
      • Open the app’s address in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. It works offline after that.
      • Photos, recordings and the word list stay on the iPad only. Packs are on the public internet, so keep family photos out of them.
      • To lock the iPad to this app: Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access, then triple-click the top button while in the app.
