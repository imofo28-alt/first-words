    Packs

    A topic of pictures, ready to add. You record the voice for each word.

      Import your own photos and recordings

      Name each file after the word — apple.jpg, apple.m4a — choose them all, and tap Import. Recordings added later attach to the matching words.

      Session

      How he moves between pictures

      Keep 3–6 words in rotation per topic. Short and daily beats long and rare. To stop a “keep going” session, hold two fingers on the screen.

      Help

      Using it with him
      • Together, on your lap. The app is a picture book; you are the teacher.
      • He swipes or taps; the app says the word in your voice. There are no wrong answers.
      • In the pause after the word, say it too. Afterwards, find the real thing: “Your ball! Same!”
      • Tick “He says it” once he uses a word away from the iPad.
      Adding words
      • Packs: tap “Add to his words”, then “● Record” on each word (say it twice, slow and warm), and tick “In rotation” on 3–6 of them.
      • One word at a time: “+ Add a word” with a photo of the real thing.
      • Packs are on the public internet. Keep family photos out of them.
      On the iPad
      • Open the app’s address in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. It works offline after that.
      • Photos, recordings and the word list stay on the iPad only.
      • To lock the iPad to this app: Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access, then triple-click the top button while in the app.
